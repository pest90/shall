# Platform capabilities

## Overtemperature protection
`@@cap~overtemp-protection@@`

The platform shuts down the motor when the winding temperature exceeds
a configurable limit, within a configurable time.

Rationale:
An example to start from: replace it with your first capability, and
create further IDs with `shall new cap "<title>" --file reqs/capabilities.md`.
Customer projects name it in `Satisfied-by:`; the platform does not
know them.
