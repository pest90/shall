# L2 – System Requirements

## Thermal protection of the motor
`sys~motor.thermal-protection-q7m2~1`

The drive protects the motor winding against overheating.

Rationale:
Overheating destroys the winding insulation.

Status: approved
Source: Customer specification ch. 4.2

Needs: swr

## Emergency stop
`sys~motor.emergency-stop-h3x9~1`

The drive stops the motor when the emergency stop input is active.

Status: approved
Source: Customer specification ch. 5.1

Needs: swr
